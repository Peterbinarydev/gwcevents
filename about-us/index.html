Global Wealth Conference

Global Wealth Conference (GWC) is a three-day gathering hosted in partnership with the City of London, held at iconic locations such as the UK Parliament and the historic Great Hall of Guildhall, in the month of May.
This annual event brings together Global Wealth Leaders to engage in insightful discussions and share global investment strategies and opportunities in alignment with the current global economic and financial landscape.
GWC conducts an information driven forum where delegates share insights, engage in forward looking discussions.

"GOOD FELLOWS" AT GLOBAL WEALTH CONFERENCE

Initiated by SWFI, the Global Order Of Outstanding Disruptors (GOOD) Fellows is a non-profit endeavor and a tribute to the ‘Modern Greats of Wealth’, the audacious and visionary individuals who, through innovation, integrity, and commitment, are redefining the narrative of wealth.
– Thought Leadership In The Global Wealth Landscape
– Knowledge Exchange Among The Brightest Minds
– Responsible & Sustainable Wealth Practices
– Positive Social Impact Through Conversations

Explore Good Fellows
An integral part of the felicitation is the GOOD Talk. This is a 15-minute interactive dialog between each award recipient and the SWFI Chairman, Lakshmi Narayanan. Here the recipients share their vision and experiences, offering insights into their journey and the motivations behind their impactful work. The idea is to not only celebrate their achievements but also serve to inspire the audience, reinforcing the importance of using wealth and influence for the greater good.

Becoming a co-host for the Global Wealth Conference offers numerous benefits to organizations looking to align themselves with global leadership in sovereign investment. Co-hosts gain exclusive access to high-profile decision-makers, policymakers, and thought leaders from around the world. Co-hosts will be recognized for their commitment to sustainability, innovation, and global economic growth, gaining exposure through event promotions and materials.
