Global Wealth Conference (GWC) by the Sovereign Wealth Fund Institute (SWFI) is a premier gathering hosted in partnership with the City of London. This annual event brings together Global Wealth Leaders to engage in insightful discussions, share global investment strategies, and access opportunities in alignment with the current global economic and financial landscape.

Global Asset Owners In The New Industrial Strategy

In a world reshaped by disruption, digitization, and decarbonization, the theme “Growth and Connectivity” reflects a pivotal moment for global asset owners. As sovereign wealth funds, family offices, and institutional investors take center stage in funding the new industrial era, GWC 2025 explored how capital can accelerate strategic growth while fostering connectivity—across sectors, borders, and systems.

The GOOD Fellows Initiative
